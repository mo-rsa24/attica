## Art direction

Navigation: 📋 [TOC](#table-of-contents) | [Next](#meaning-palette) ➡️

**Outline cartoon.** Attica's subject is people coordinating with each other, not services passing
traffic, so warmth reads better than engineering precision here. It also gives the side-by-side band
layout that two of these prompts need.

The direction's style paragraph is embedded verbatim at the head of every prompt below, so any
single prompt survives being pasted on its own.

## Meaning palette

Navigation: ⬅️ [Art direction](#art-direction) | 📋 [TOC](#table-of-contents) | [Next](#glyph-vocabulary) ➡️

Four colors, each carrying exactly one meaning, the same in every image.

| Color | Means |
|---|---|
| Warm coral | the organizer, and anything on the buying side |
| Teal | a provider, and anything on the selling side |
| Soft gold | an event, the occasion everything serves |
| Muted gray | something that exists but is not live: a draft, a vestigial table, an unbuilt half |

## Glyph vocabulary

Navigation: ⬅️ [Meaning palette](#meaning-palette) | 📋 [TOC](#table-of-contents) | [Next](#reading-axes) ➡️

| Glyph | Stands for | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Cartoon person with a clipboard | the organizer | primary |
| Building with an arched door | a venue | primary |
| Microphone with a small figure | an artist | primary |
| Van with a tray | a vendor (service provider) | primary |
| Gold calendar card | an event | primary |
| Rounded card outline, empty | a marketplace resource, the wrapper around a provider | secondary |
| Handshake badge | a booking, meaning a commitment | artifact |
| Speech bubble with a price tag | a bid | artifact |
| Ticket stub | a ticket | artifact |
| Faded dashed card | anything vestigial or unbuilt | secondary |

## Reading axes

Navigation: ⬅️ [Glyph vocabulary](#glyph-vocabulary) | 📋 [TOC](#table-of-contents) | [Next](#devices-in-play) ➡️

**Dominant axis: left to right**, meaning time or progression in the process lane and generality to
specificity in the subject lane. **Secondary axis: vertical**, encoding which side of the
marketplace something sits on, with the buying side above and the selling side below.
